## 3. Brand Philosophy and Values
Nine Labs Solutions is a bridge between brands and their results, and the website should express that idea from the first screen.
Our philosophy: we are the bridge. A bridge only works when traffic moves in both directions. We don’t see clients as accounts to serve and invoice. We see your company as our own, and your results as our results. Your goals reach us, our expertise reaches you, and together we get across.
Our values
- Partnership: we work with you, not just for you. Your ideas and feedback matter as much as our strategy.
- Ownership: your business is treated as our own, and we stand behind the results.
- Trust: honest advice, clear reporting and no hidden surprises.
- Quality: every campaign, video, event and strategy is built to a standard we would put our own name on.
- Loyalty: we stay with clients for the long run, and we grow when they grow.

## 5. Homepage Design Description
### 5.1 Overall look and feel
The homepage should feel modern and futuristic while staying creative. A pure black screen with a glowing electric blue 9 and neon light purple accents gives the futuristic look, and the faint blueprint grid behind everything says we plan and build with precision. The site should feel bold and different from typical agency websites, but clean and easy to use.
### 5.2 Colour and background
Element
Direction
Background
Black across the whole page. Pure black or very near black (exact code to be supplied).
The 9 (hero colour)
Electric blue, suggested around #3B82FF (reference only, exact code to be supplied). Used for the 9, its glow and the nine activity elements. Blue sits next to purple on the colour wheel, so the two blend well, and it echoes classic blueprint blue. Keep the glow soft so text stays easy to read.
Supporting accent
Neon light purple, used for buttons, links, highlights, card hover glows and the bridge line. A soft blue-to-purple glow can sit behind the 9. Exact code to be supplied.
Blueprint grid
A very light white square grid behind the content, like blueprint paper. It should be barely visible, roughly 4 to 8 percent white on black, so it adds depth without competing with the content (reference only, to be tuned in design).
Text
White for headings, light grey for body text. Check that all text and the blue and purple elements are readable on black.
Other colours
None needed beyond electric blue and neon purple. If a lighter or deeper shade of either is wanted, propose it in your concepts.
### 5.3 Typography
Strong, modern headings in a clean sans-serif or a slightly technical typeface, with a simple, highly readable body font. Large headings with plenty of space around them. Please propose the font pairing in your design concepts.
### 5.4 The cover: the 9 and the nine activities
This is the signature element of the homepage.
- The 9: a large 9 sits in the exact center of the screen as the hero. It is drawn in electric blue with a soft blue glow that can fade into neon light purple at the edges. Please propose how it is built (for example, a glowing outline, a 3D shape or a continuous line drawing). It should feel alive, with a gentle pulse or slow shimmer.
- The carousel: the nine activities come around the 9 like a carousel. Each activity appears as an image or graphic element (see the suggestions in section 4.3) with its name beside it. The elements travel along a circular or oval path around the 9, and all nine pass through in turn.
- The focus moment: the element at the front of the path becomes larger and brighter, and its name and one-line description appear clearly. The others stay smaller and dimmer behind and beside the 9.
- Speed and control: the movement is slow and smooth. It pauses when the visitor hovers over an element, and clicking an element takes the visitor to that activity in the Services section. On mobile, the visitor can swipe to move the carousel.
- Text on the cover: the headline, sub-headline and the two buttons sit clearly on the screen without being covered by the moving elements. A suggested layout is the headline and buttons below the 9, but please propose what works best.
- The bridge: a fine neon purple line or arc can connect the activities to the 9, so the screen quietly shows the bridge idea from section 3.
### 5.5 The blueprint grid
- The grid sits behind the whole cover and can continue, more faintly, behind the sections below.
- Optional blueprint details in the same very light white: small corner marks, thin measurement ticks or small plus signs at some grid crossings.
- The grid may drift very slowly or react slightly to mouse movement, but never enough to distract.
### 5.6 Sections below the cover
- Services: the nine activities again as nine cards on the black background, each with the visual element, name and short description. Cards glow in neon purple on hover.
- About and values: a short About text with five value cards. A thin bridge line can run between the About text and the values as a divider.
- The 9 Quiz: a full-width block with a large heading, one line of text and the main button, with a faint purple glow behind it.
- Free e-books: two or three book cover cards in a row with download buttons.
- Contact and footer: a closing call to action, then a simple footer on black with the contact details and social links.
- Sections appear with smooth fade-and-rise animation as the visitor scrolls. Section dividers use the bridge motif.
### 5.7 Buttons, links and interaction
- Main button (Start The 9 Quiz): filled neon purple with black or white text, whichever is more readable, and a soft glow.
- Second button (Download our free e-books): outlined in neon purple with a transparent center.
- Hover and focus states on every button and link, with a clear glow or underline.
### 5.8 Motion and speed
- All motion is smooth and subtle. The cover animation must not slow the page: please explain how it will be built (for example, CSS, canvas or WebGL) and its effect on loading time.
- Target a fast first load, with images and graphics optimized.
- Respect visitors who prefer reduced motion by showing a still version of the cover.
### 5.9 Mobile and responsiveness
- The homepage must work well on phones, tablets and desktops.
- On mobile, the 9 stays in the center and the carousel becomes a smaller, swipeable ring or row. Text and buttons stack below the 9 and stay easy to tap.
### 5.10 Inspiration and originality
The homepage of theninelabs.com shows a 9 with elements coming across it, and we like that idea. It belongs to a separate company with a similar name, so please use it as inspiration only. Our design, graphics, layout and wording must be our own, and the electric blue 9, the blueprint grid and the bridge idea are what make ours different.
